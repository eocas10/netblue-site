Cybersecurity services for businesses in Puerto Rico

A ransomware attack or a fake email can stop your business for days. We protect your network, your devices and your data before an incident costs you money and clients.

The most common threats to a small business

  • Phishing: fake emails and messages that steal passwords or redirect payments (BEC fraud).
  • Ransomware: programs that encrypt your files and demand a ransom to return them (see it in the simulator).
  • Stolen or reused passwords that open the door to your email and business systems.
  • Outdated devices and routers with factory passwords (see our patching guide).

What our service includes

  • Vulnerability assessments with a written remediation plan.
  • Firewall with intrusion prevention (IPS/IDS) and network segmentation.
  • Managed antivirus and mobile device management (MDM).
  • Email security and two-step verification (MFA).
  • Encryption of computers, laptops and data.
  • Off-site backups and a disaster recovery plan.
  • Security awareness training for your employees, with our simulator lab and anti-phishing practices.

How we start

First we assess your network, devices and email. We give you a clear report of what is fine, what is missing and what it costs to fix, in order of priority. You decide what to do and when.

Frequently asked questions

Is a small business really a target for attacks?

Yes. Attackers use automated tools that look for any vulnerable device or account, no matter the size of the business. Small businesses tend to have less protection, which makes them easier to attack.

What should I protect first?

Two-step verification on email, off-site backups and keeping devices updated. They are low-cost measures that prevent most of the incidents we see.

Do you offer employee training?

Yes. We offer security awareness training so your staff learns to recognize fake emails and other scams.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Both. We serve businesses all across the island: many tasks are done remotely and installations are done at your location.
